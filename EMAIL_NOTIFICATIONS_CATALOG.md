# ScholarHub HPU — Comprehensive Email & Notification Catalog
*(ScholarSync & ScholarTrack Portals)*

> **System Overview**: All email notifications across **ScholarSync** and **ScholarTrack** are managed centrally via MongoDB-backed durable queue (`EmailQueue` model) and processed sequentially by `emailWorker.js` at ~3.3 emails/sec via Stalwart SMTP server (`admin@scholarhubhpu.in`).
> When any system event creates a `Notification` document, the Mongoose `post('save')` hook automatically triggers `queueNotificationEmail`, generating an HTML email formatted with portal-specific branding (Forest Green `#133A26` for ScholarSync and Mint/Teal `#0F766E` for ScholarTrack).
